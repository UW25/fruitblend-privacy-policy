Privacy Policy

App: FruitBlend  •  Last updated: [SEPTEMBER 8, 2026]

This Privacy Policy explains how ReveraSolutions (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information in connection with the mobile game FruitBlend (the “App”). By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect or store personal information on our own servers. The App has no user account forms. Your game progress, settings, and a device-scoped game identifier are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us by the App.

2. Third-Party Services (Advertising)

The App displays ads through Google AdMob, a service provided by Google. To serve ads, Google and its partners may automatically collect and share certain information, such as your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, general device information, app interaction data, and diagnostic information. This information may be used for advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance, including delivering and measuring ads where permitted.

We do not have access to, and do not receive, this information ourselves.

You can review how Google uses this data here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

3. Consent for Personalized Ads

Google determines whether consent or other privacy controls are required for advertising based on your region and applicable law. Where available, you can manage advertising personalization through Google and your Android device settings. Personalized advertising is served only where permitted by the applicable consent and privacy controls.

4. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

5. Data Security

We do not store your game data or advertising data on our own servers. Advertising data transmitted by Google’s Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using industry-standard transport security. Google handles advertising data in accordance with its own security practices.

6. Your Choices

  • You can manage advertising personalization and reset or delete your advertising identifier through your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy / Google → Ads on Android).
  • You can uninstall the App to stop all further data processing by third-party ad services.
